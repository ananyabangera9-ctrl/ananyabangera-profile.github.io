The question

Businesses are moving from “take-make-dispose” to a circular economy (CE), where materials are reused and recycled. Does that shift, and owning scarce landfills, actually pay off for shareholders?

The gap

Most CE research is conceptual. ESG studies use blended scores that can’t isolate one strategy, and the financial role of physical assets like landfills is under-studied (Pomponi & Moncaster, 2016).

The answer: a “qualified yes”

Landfills form a durable competitive moat. Circular economy helps mainly as a complement to the core business model, not as a standalone driver of valuation.

Research design

A comparative case study using secondary data, with patterns observed rather than causation claimed (Yin, 2018).

ElementWhat I used
Independent variablesCE strategy (recycling revenue as a share of revenue) · Landfill ownership (number of landfills; DD&A as a share of revenue as a capital-intensity proxy)

Evidence of vertical integration

About 42% of Republic Services’ 2024 landfill revenue was intercompany ($1,240M of $2,981M): its own collection trucks feed its own landfills, the captive-disposal advantage described by Transaction Cost Economics.

Key takeaways

Two successful models, and CE as a complement.

1. Asset-heavy model (WM, RSG)

Value comes from what the firm owns: scarce landfills, vertical integration, stable margins and strong long-run returns.

2. Agility-led model (WCN)

Value comes from what the market believes the firm can do: acquisition-led growth earns a premium multiple despite the lowest ROE.

3. CE is a complement

Recycling revenue did not by itself predict a premium valuation. It adds value by strengthening the existing business model.

Practical implication: investors who rely only on environmental scores risk flawed valuations. They should look at assets, strategy and financial materiality together.

Limitations and what I’d do differently

Part of good research is knowing where it is weakest.

IssueWhat I’d change
Only three firms, self-reported public data, no causal claimsWiden to more firms and regions, then run panel regressions
CE disclosure is inconsistent across firmsStandardise the CE measure on one basis. This page uses recycling revenue for all three; the dissertation text cites 11.89% for Republic, which reflects its wider Environmental Solutions segment (2.55% on a recycling basis)
Data validationRe-verify the WM EV/EBITDA series in the appendix, which duplicates its EV/Sales row, before drawing valuation comparisons
CE revenue only, with no costsSeparate CE revenue from CE costs to estimate true returns
